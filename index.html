Our Mission

🚫 The Problem

While large cities can afford $50K-$250K enterprise data platforms, 95% of America's municipalities rely on spreadsheets and intuition to make critical decisions about their communities. This civic intelligence gap leaves smaller cities, nonprofits, and community organizations without the insights they need to secure funding and serve their residents effectively.

✅ Our Solution

CommunityInsight.AI democratizes access to powerful civic analytics through ethical AI. Our platform makes sophisticated community intelligence accessible to every municipality—regardless of size or budget. With 5 autonomous AI agents and integration with federal data APIs, we provide enterprise-level insights at a fraction of the cost.
